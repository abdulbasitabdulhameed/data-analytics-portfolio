# Olist Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive sales performance dashboard built in **Power BI** using the public **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce** dataset.

The analysis focuses on revenue, orders, customers, product categories, payment methods, and customer locations to understand sales performance and identify the strongest areas of the business.

## Business Problem

The business needed to understand:

- How is overall sales performance?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which product categories contribute most to revenue and orders?
- Which states have the largest customer base?
- Which payment methods contribute most to revenue?
- Where is sales activity most concentrated?

## About the Dataset

The **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce** dataset contains transactional data from a Brazilian online marketplace, including orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, and reviews.

The related tables provide a practical foundation for analysing sales performance across products, customers, orders, locations, payments, and time.

**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset  
**Source:** Kaggle

## Approach

The data was prepared, modelled, and analysed using **Power BI**.

**Power Query**

- Cleaned and transformed the source tables
- Reviewed and corrected data types
- Prepared the data for analysis

**Data Modelling & DAX**

- Established relationships between the relevant Olist tables
- Structured the data model for sales analysis
- Created measures for revenue, orders, customers, and average order value

**Power BI**

- Built an interactive sales performance dashboard
- Analysed revenue trends by year
- Compared product categories by revenue and order volume
- Analysed customer distribution by state
- Compared revenue by payment type
- Added interactive filters for month, category, and state

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Growth

Revenue increased substantially between 2016 and 2018, indicating strong growth in marketplace sales activity over the period.

### 2. Geographic Concentration

Customer activity was concentrated in a small number of states, with São Paulo clearly leading the other states shown in the dashboard.

### 3. Product Performance

The leading product categories contributed a significant share of overall revenue, while order volume was also concentrated among a similar group of categories.

### 4. Payment Behaviour

Credit card generated the largest share of revenue among the payment methods analysed, making it the dominant payment channel in the dashboard.

### 5. Sales Concentration

Sales performance was concentrated across a relatively small number of product categories and customer markets, highlighting the areas with the greatest commercial importance.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, the business should:

1. **Prioritise high performing product categories** by maintaining availability and investigating the factors driving stronger revenue and order performance.

2. **Focus sales and marketing analysis on major customer markets**, particularly the strongest states, while investigating opportunities in lower performing locations.

3. **Maintain strong support for major payment methods**, particularly credit cards, while monitoring changes in customer payment preferences.

4. **Compare revenue and order performance together** to distinguish categories driven by high order volume from those generating higher value per order.

5. **Monitor revenue growth over time** to support sales planning and identify changes in marketplace performance.

## Business Impact

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance across revenue, orders, customers, products, locations, and payment methods.

The analysis helps identify where sales activity is concentrated and where management attention may be required.
